I'm Luci; a portrait photographer based in northeast Ohio. I specialize in equine photography but I also have experience with families, couples, engagements, seniors, pets, and more!

Do you ever wish that life had a pause button so that you could savor those special moments just a little longer? Book a session today! I would love to capture your milestones and help you create lasting memories that you and your family can cherish forever!
